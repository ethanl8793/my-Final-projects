Breakfast: Pancakes

Ingredients

  1. 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  2. 3 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  3. 1 ¼ cups milk
  4. 3 tablespoons butter, melted
  5. 1 large egg

Instructions

  1. Scoop or pour the batter into the pan to take up about 80% of the pan.
  2. Cook until the batter on top has solidified into bubbles and flip.

Toppings

  1. Enjoy with maple syrup, whipped cream, fruit, etc.

Lunch: Salmon and Salad

Ingredients

  1. 10 to 12 oz cooked salmon or 2 cans drained salmon
  2. Chopped celery, red onion, and green onions
  3. Fresh dill or parsley
  4. Dressing: mayo or Greek yogurt + lemon juice + Dijon mustard

Instructions

  1. Bake salmon at 400°F for 8 to 12 minutes until flaky, or use canned salmon.
  2. Whisk lemon juice, mustard, and mayo/yogurt.
  3. Mix salmon, vegetables, and herbs into the dressing.
  4. Serve on bread, lettuce wraps, or greens.

Toppings

  1. Croutons, balsamic vinegar, etc.

Dinner: Steak and Mashed Potatoes

Ingredients

  1. 4 medium potatoes (peeled and cubed)
  2. 6 tbsp butter
  3. 1/2 cup milk or cream
  4. Salt and pepper
  5. 1 lb sirloin or ribeye steak (cut into cubes)
  6. Olive oil
  7. 3 cloves minced garlic

Instructions

Potatoes

  1. Boil potatoes 15 minutes until soft.
  2. Drain, add butter/milk/salt/pepper, mash until smooth.

Steak

  1. Season steak with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
  2. Sear in hot oil for 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Add butter + garlic, stir for 30 seconds.

Toppings

  1. Gravy, salad, etc.
